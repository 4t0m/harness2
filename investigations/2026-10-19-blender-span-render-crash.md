# Span preview crash

Expected: preserve accepted spans and render two sparse previews. Observed: original 12 frames completed, then Blender exited -11 before revised frames. Crash stack points to dependency graph refresh / collection free during ViewLayer.update. Source unchanged; revised.blend was saved before rendering. No earlier attempts in this span trial.

Hypotheses: corrupt revised scene (fresh load/snapshot/render should fail); mutating animation after rendering destabilizes evaluated scene state (fresh process with immutable revised scene should pass); explicit repeated view-layer refresh is involved independently (snapshot-only fresh loads may fail, or render-only versus update distinguishes it). These are not mutually exclusive; don't infer an upstream platform change.

Diagnostic: fresh process loads source and snapshots all object transforms for 48 frames into plain data, loads saved revised scene and repeats. Verify outside-frame/other-object preservation and source hash; persist before render. Render revised frames without changing animation curves, in a new output child. If it passes, this rules out an unusable revised asset and shows a working isolation strategy; it does not distinguish every Blender memory-lifetime cause. Retain crash and original partial artifacts, no overwritten frames. Cap process to 100 seconds.

Fresh-process revised rendering succeeded and full 48-frame snapshots proved only frames 13–35 changed, with all non-arm objects unchanged. However independently encoded original/revised videos had identical SHA-256. The first original preview was rendered after editing/restoring a curve in-process and is therefore an unreliable baseline (likely stale evaluated render data; exact mechanism unproven). Do not present it as before/after. Additional diagnostic: render original source in a fresh process without any curve mutation and compare decoded image hashes against revised. Keep the first pair as failed comparison artifacts.

Isolated original succeeded. Decoded RGB hashes are equal at sampled source frames 1/5/9/37/41/45 and unequal at 13/17/21/25/29/33. Frame25 visual inspection shows original arm higher and revised arm lower. This confirms usable saved scenes and a working isolated-render strategy. The in-process edit/render baseline is invalid; no precise Blender internal root-cause claim. Retain the crashing script as a reproducer, not the recommended execution path. Source and revised scenes are independently loaded for trustworthy comparisons.
