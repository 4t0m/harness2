# Creative director and art-production manager task family

User added this direction explicitly: a custom small model can manage an aspect of art creation, not only actuate an avatar. Character design ownership stays with the creative workshop; this lab owns reusable harness contracts and evidence.

## Useful roles

- Brief steward: extract immutable requirements, editable preferences, unresolved questions and prohibited changes.
- Variant planner: request a deliberately diverse small audition set; change one dimension at a time when comparison matters.
- Production coordinator: track jobs, dependencies, retry budgets, artifact IDs, revisions and provenance; never confuse queued with generated.
- Critique organizer: tie observations to actual artifacts; separate compliance failures from taste judgments and uncertain visual inferences.
- Revision manager: preserve accepted traits and edit only the chosen dimensions; prepare a new revision without overwriting its parent.
- Casting assistant: match a design to available rig capabilities, viewpoints and required poses; distinguish attractive concept art from rig-ready layers.

## First cheap fixture

A synthetic brief specifies blue coat, no logo and three silhouette variations, with pose fixed. Model proposes a three-item job plan referencing a known tool capability and immutable brief revision. Validator checks the constraints and variation coverage, duplicate IDs, budget, allowed transitions and missing dependencies. Then inject one failed job and ask the model to retry only it while preserving completed artifacts. No actual generation is needed to test the management logic.

A second fixture provides prewritten critique claims linked to artifact IDs and asks for a revision queue: missing evidence stays unverified, hard brief violations are prioritized, and conflicting taste preferences remain alternatives. Do not score artistic quality by counting checklist items.

## Model/harness experiment

Use the same task families for Luna, Sol and eventual local models. Compare baseline prompt, bounded repair and Harness² refinement. Freeze job-state rules outside editable harness surfaces. Preserve initial errors and repair evidence. Any actual image generation belongs behind an explicitly configured tool route with a per-job cost/time cap, not a generic shell instruction.

Current boundary: these are new fixture designs, not demonstrated creative competence or a qualified art-generation integration. The creative workshop has independently reported a working Antigravity route; actual cross-harness integration remains untested.
